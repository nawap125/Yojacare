Add YOJA Care to your Home Screen

Do this first, so your photos and progress stay with the app icon.

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow) in Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add, then open YOJA Care from your Home Screen.

This opens your Share menu. Choose Add to Home Screen there.

This looks like an in-app browser. Tap the menu (…) or the compass icon and choose Open in Safari first.
